Why this matters

College success isn't just about willpower. It's about knowing when and where to reach out. The students who struggle most aren't always the ones who don't know things. They're the ones who wait too long to ask.

This module helps you build your support map: the people and resources you can actually use when things get hard. That network is one of the smartest investments you can make right now.

What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module:

  1. A

    Objective A: I can identify people and OCTC resources that can support my success and explain how each one helps.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #2
  2. B

    Objective B: I can describe how I will use my support system intentionally this semester.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #2
  3. C

    Objective C: I can identify one new campus resource I want to explore and explain why it matters for my goals.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #2
  4. D

    Objective D: I can practice asking for help clearly and specifically in a realistic college scenario.

    You'll practice this in your course activities
