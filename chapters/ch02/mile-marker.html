Mile Marker

Mile Marker #2: My Support Team

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 10 minutes 7 of 8 in this module

No one completes a journey alone. This Mile Marker is about identifying the people and resources around you, and thinking about how to use them intentionally.

You'll come back to this reflection when you build your capstone project.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to explore your ideas. Work with what it gives you, then write your responses in your own voice.

See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus. Want a place to start? Try the optional AI thinking partner prompt.

Jump to the fill-in form

Respond to each question

Question 1

Who are 3–5 people or resources that can help you succeed this semester?

Think about family, friends, mentors, coworkers, instructors, advisors, tutors, your Success Coach, TRIO, Counseling, the Library, and other OCTC resources. For each one, explain specifically how they can support you.

A strong answer

Names at least three people or resources, explains specifically how each one helps, and includes your Success Coach by name.

Question 2

What are two specific ways you'll use your support network intentionally this semester?

Describe two specific strategies. Not just "I'll reach out when I need help," but when, how, and for what. Which connections do you want to strengthen?

A strong answer

Describes two specific ways you'll use your network, each with when, how, and for what you'll reach out. It shows the difference between knowing support exists and actually using it.

Look back at

Lesson 2.3: Four tips for confident help-seeking

Question 3

What is one new campus resource you want to explore?

Consider the Counseling Center, Accessibility Services, Advising, your Success Coach, the Library, the Teaching & Learning Center (tutoring), TRIO, Student Life and clubs, Career Services, or Financial Aid. Choose one that feels relevant to your goals or situation right now, and explain why.

A strong answer

Names a resource you haven't used yet, explains what it offers and how it connects to your goals, and names one concrete next step for getting there.

Optional: AI thinking partner

If you'd like help thinking through your answers before you write, try this prompt in any AI tool:

Try this prompt

"Ask me questions to help me identify the people and resources in my life that support my success as a college student, and how I can use them more intentionally this semester."

Use what's useful. Your final response must be in your own words.

AI isn't a substitute for people

AI tools can help you brainstorm and organize your thinking, but they are not a substitute for human support. If you're dealing with stress, motivation challenges, or anything personal, reach out to your instructor, your Success Coach, or the OCTC Counseling Center (opens in a new tab). Not an AI tool.

How it's scored

Strong responses answer every question completely, with specific detail, in your own words, using complete sentences and a college-appropriate tone.
