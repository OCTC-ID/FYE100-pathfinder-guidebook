Why this matters

Let's be honest: group work has a reputation, and not always a great one. This module isn't about pretending it's easy. It's about figuring out how you work best, so you can say so, instead of just hoping things go okay.

You'll take the 16Personalities assessment, look at your collaboration strengths and struggles, and pick up strategies that fit how you operate. All of it feeds straight into Mile Marker #10.

Merit reminder: Merit Activity #3 is due with this module

Find an activity, then submit your reflection to the Merit Activity #3 link in Blackboard. Check Blackboard for the due date. The Merit Guide has your options, what proof to include, and how to write your reflection.

What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module:

  1. A

    Objective A: I can identify my natural collaboration tendencies using my 16Personalities results and explain how they show up in group work.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #10
  2. B

    Objective B: I can name two collaboration strengths and one or two challenges and explain why those challenges happen.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #10
  3. C

    Objective C: I can describe three specific strategies that help me work more effectively with others.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #10
  4. D

    Objective D: I can articulate in one clear sentence what I need from teammates to do my best work.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #10
