Why this matters

Most students who struggle academically aren't lazy. They're overwhelmed, under-rested, and running on empty, and they don't realize it until things start falling apart.

This module asks you to look honestly at where your time and energy are actually going: not the week you wish you had, but the one you actually live. That's the only honest starting point for building something better.

What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module:

  1. A

    Objective A: I can analyze how I currently spend my time and identify at least one pattern or time-waster.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #3
  2. B

    Objective B: I can create a realistic weekly schedule that reflects my actual priorities and commitments.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #3
  3. C

    Objective C: I can identify which of my five energy batteries needs the most attention and describe one way I will recharge it.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #3
  4. D

    Objective D: I can describe one small habit or adjustment I will make to manage my time more effectively.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #3
