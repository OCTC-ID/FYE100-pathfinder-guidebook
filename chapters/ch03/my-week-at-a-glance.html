Plan from your priorities, not from empty boxes.

This planner walks you through the three steps from Lesson 3.2 in order: name your priorities, know your energy, then block your week. Use what your time log showed you. If mornings kept slipping away or evenings were your best hours, plan for that.

Pathfinder Tip

This is a tool, not a contract. Plans change. The point isn't a perfect week. It's starting with intention instead of reacting to whatever shows up.
