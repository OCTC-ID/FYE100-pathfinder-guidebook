Where My Time Goes

Look back: an honest log of where your time and energy actually go.

About 5 minutes 1 of 10 in this module

Before you can plan your time well, you need an honest picture of where it's actually going. Not the week you meant to have. The one you really had.

This tracker is a reality check. For each day, jot down what you actually did in the morning, afternoon, and evening, and how your energy felt. It takes a few minutes at the end of the day. Nobody grades this log, and there are no wrong answers.

Pathfinder Tip

Start today. Aim for a full week. If your Mile Marker is due sooner, log as many days as you can. Even three honest days will show you something.

How to log honestly

  • Write what happened, not what you planned. "Meant to study, ended up on my phone" is more useful than "study."
  • Keep it short. A few words per block is plenty: "work shift," "class, then errands," "TV and scrolling."
  • Check your energy. Low, OK, or high. This is how you'll find your best hours.
  • Log it the same day. By tomorrow, you'll remember the day you meant to have.
