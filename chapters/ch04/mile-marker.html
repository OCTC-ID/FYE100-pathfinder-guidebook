Mile Marker #4: My Academic Toolkit

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 10 minutes 7 of 8 in this module

Every Pathfinder needs the right tools: not someone else's toolkit, but the ones that actually work for how you learn.

This Mile Marker is about figuring out what that looks like for you. Not what worked for a classmate, not what a study guide says, but what works for you, in your real life, this term.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to explore your ideas. Work with what it gives you, then write your responses in your own voice, including your answer to Question 4.

See the AI Course Policy in the syllabus. Want a place to start? Try the optional AI thinking partner prompt.

Respond to each question

Question 1

What study strategy, habit, or academic tool did you focus on this week, and why did you choose it?

Be specific about what it is and what made it worth trying. "I studied more" isn't specific. "I tried reviewing my notes out loud the night before class" is.

A strong answer

Names one specific strategy, habit, or tool and gives a real reason you chose it.

Question 2

How did you actually use it, and what did you notice?

Did it help you understand, remember, or stay focused? Did anything surprise you, good or bad? Be honest about what happened, not what you hoped would happen.

A strong answer

Describes how you used it in a real class or task and at least one specific thing you noticed about your learning.

Look back at

The "Try it and notice" boxes in Lesson 4.2

Question 3

Write one SMART goal for keeping a study strategy or habit from this module going for the rest of the term.

This isn't asking for a perfect plan. It's asking for one honest goal that fits where you are right now. Make it:

  • Specific: what exactly will you do?
  • Measurable: how will you know you did it?
  • Achievable: why is it realistic for your life right now?
  • Relevant: why this habit, and which class or goal does it help?
  • Time-bound: when, and how often?
A strong answer

Covers all five parts, names a real class or goal, and includes a clear way to tell whether you followed through.

Question 4

Think about any AI tool you used this week, for this course or any other. Where would your use land on the AI Creep Meter?

Closer to brainstorming, closer to drafting or rewriting, or somewhere in between? Was that where you meant to land, or did it shift further than you planned? If you didn't use AI at all this week, that's a complete answer too. Just say so.

A strong answer

Says honestly where your AI use landed (or that you didn't use AI) and whether that was where you meant to land. Full credit doesn't depend on where you landed on the meter.

How it's scored

Full credit goes to responses that are specific, honest, and self-aware, written in your own voice using complete sentences and a college-appropriate tone. Generic answers that could apply to anyone won't earn full credit.

For Question 4, full credit doesn't require that your AI use stayed at one stage. It requires honest reflection on where it landed and whether that was intentional.
