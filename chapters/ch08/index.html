Why this matters

A good career fit takes more than choosing something that "sounds cool" or "pays well." It means finding work that lines up with who you are, what you value, what you're good at, and how you actually want to live.

In this module you'll use real research tools to explore one career in depth, and honestly assess whether it fits. You'll also set up your Handshake profile and get ready to register for next semester.

What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module:

  1. A

    Objective A: I can research one career using Career Coach and O*NET, including regional salary, job outlook, education requirements, and typical work environment.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #8
  2. B

    Objective B: I can evaluate a career for personal fit by connecting my research findings to my strengths, values, work style, and pressure tolerance.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #8
  3. C

    Objective C: I can articulate one clear question I still have about this career direction.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #8
