Career Research Guide

One career, researched well, and an honest look at the fit.

About 30 minutes 1 of 7 in this module

One career, researched well. Then an honest look at whether it fits you.

Work through the five sections in order. The first three are research: what the job is, what the numbers say, and what the work is really like. The last two are where it gets personal. When you're done, save it as a PDF and upload it with Mile Marker #8.

Look ahead

Lesson 8.2: Research Your Career shows you how to use Career Coach and O*NET for each section.

Fill in your research

Your answers save in this browser as you go. Select Save as PDF when you're finished, then upload the PDF to the Mile Marker #8 assignment in Blackboard.

Research sources

  • Career Coach (opens in a new tab): regional salary, job outlook, and OCTC programs
  • O*NET OnLine (opens in a new tab): tasks, skills, work styles, and work context
  • Occupational Outlook Handbook (opens in a new tab): national pay and outlook
  • Job postings on Handshake, Indeed, or Glassdoor: what employers are actually asking for
