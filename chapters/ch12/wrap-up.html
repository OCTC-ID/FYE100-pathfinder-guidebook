Wrap Up

Module 12 Wrap Up

You've walked the whole trail.

About 2 minutes 5 of 5 in this module

You've walked the whole trail.

You started this semester reflecting on where you were. You're finishing it with four true stories about what you can do, in your own voice.

What you're taking with you

  • A support team. People and places to go when things get hard, and the words to ask.
  • A way to plan your time and energy. Map the week, and watch your batteries.
  • A plan and a direction. Your program, your next courses, and a career you've researched.
  • Words for who you are. Your strengths, your values, how you work with others, and what you bring to your communities.
  • Tools for thinking. SIFT for information, the 4C Check for decisions, and the judgment to use AI on your own terms.
  • Your stories. Four STAR(T) Stories you can tell in an interview, a scholarship conversation, or any time someone asks, "Tell me about a time when..."
One more thing

In Blackboard, take the My Skills Snapshot one more time and compare it to your answers from the start of the course. Where did you grow the most?

That's the trail.

The guides stay here whenever you need them: the STAR(T) Stories Guide for your next interview, and the Merit Guide for getting involved. Finish strong.
