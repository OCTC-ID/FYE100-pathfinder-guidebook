Trail Tool

Look Back

Your Mile Markers in one place, and room to notice what stands out.

About 15 minutes 1 of 5 in this module

Before you write your final reflection, take a quick look back at the trail.

You don't need to reread everything. Skim your Mile Markers, notice what stands out, and jot it down here. Then use it for Mile Marker #12 and your STAR(T) Stories.

Where are my answers?
