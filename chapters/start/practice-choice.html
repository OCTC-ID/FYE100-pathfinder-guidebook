Practice Guide: Your Choice

Choose your skill, then draft your story.

About 20 minutes 8 of 8 in this guide

The fourth story is yours. Pick the skill you can tell the most honest story about.

Three of your four stories are assigned. This one is yours to choose. Pick the skill that connects most honestly to a real experience, from this course or from your life before college. The best choice is the one where you have the most specific, honest thing to say, not the one that sounds most impressive.

This is practice, and it isn't graded. Draft your story here, see how long it runs out loud, then record it or copy it into the matching STAR(T) Stories portal in Blackboard.

STAR(T) Practice: Student Choice

Enter your name before saving.

Step 0Choose your skill
SSituationSet the scene. What was going on?

Give just enough context for someone who wasn't there. Two or three sentences is usually plenty.

Watch out: The Situation is the setup, not the story. Get to the Task quickly.

TTaskDefine your role. What was yours to do?

What were you trying to do or solve, and what part of it was yours?

Watch out: Your Task is what you were responsible for, not the group's general goal.

AActionShow what you did, step by step.

This is the most important step. Use "I": I decided, I reached out, I chose. Describe the specific things you did and the choices you made. This is where your skill lives. Show it, don't just claim it.

Watch out: "We figured it out" hides your part. Describe what you did, even inside a group effort.

RResultName the outcome. What happened because of what you did?

What changed or got resolved because of what you did? Be specific.

Watch out: "It went well" isn't a result. Name what specifically happened.

TTransferConnect it forward. What does this mean for what comes next?

Where will this skill show up next? Name a program, a kind of work, a relationship, or a responsibility where it will matter.

Watch out: "This will help me in the future" isn't a Transfer. Name a specific place, role, or situation.
