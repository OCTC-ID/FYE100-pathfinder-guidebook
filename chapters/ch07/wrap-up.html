Wrap Up

Module 7 Wrap Up

Pull it together before you head back to your course.

About 2 minutes 6 of 6 in this module

You've reached the end of Module 7. Here's what to carry forward.

Key takeaways

  • Leadership is a pattern, not a position. Contributing, adjusting, and encouraging others through change is Adaptability & Leadership at the Milestone level.
  • Values tell you who you are. Interests can shift. Values tend to stay steadier and make a more reliable compass for big decisions.
  • Notice the matches and the gaps. Where your choices line up with your values, things feel right even when they're hard. Where they don't, that friction has a name.
  • Strengths are what already works. Use them on purpose in how you study, how you work with others, and the direction you explore.
  • Every strength has a blind spot. Name yours and decide ahead of time what you'll do when it shows up.
  • AI can describe a strength. Only you can say if it fits. Check what it tells you against your real life.
Reflect

Which one of your strengths or values do you most want to use on purpose in the next few weeks, and where?
