Mile Marker #9: Think It Through

The assignment, with support, and a form you can fill in and save.

About 15 minutes 7 of 8 in this module

The thinking behind the thinking, and your first STAR(T) draft.

In this module you learned to spot thinking traps and use the 4C Check to slow down before reacting. You also saw how that same thinking makes a STAR(T) story more honest and specific. This Mile Marker has two parts: a look at your own thinking patterns, and your first STAR(T) draft, a real starting point for your capstone.

●○AI use: AI-supported

You may use AI as a thinking partner to ask you questions about your draft. It can't tell your story for you. Your responses must be in your own words and reflect your real experience.

Part I: Know your pattern

Question 1

Name Your Pattern and a Better Question

Which thinking trap or bias from this module feels most like you? Choose from jumping to conclusions, all-or-nothing thinking, overgeneralizing, mind reading, catastrophizing, confirmation bias, familiarity bias, the halo effect, or negativity bias, or describe one in your own words.

  • Give a real example of a time this pattern showed up in your life.
  • What's one question you could ask yourself instead, the next time it shows up?
  • What's one strategy that helps you slow down and think more clearly?
A strong answer

Names one pattern, gives a real example, offers a better question, and names one strategy for slowing down.

Part II: Your first STAR(T) draft

Question 2

Tell Your Story

Pick one real experience, from this term or the recent past, and write it out using all five STAR(T) steps.

  • Situation: What was going on?
  • Task: What needed to happen, and what was your role?
  • Action: What did you specifically do? Not the group. You.
  • Result: What happened?
  • Transfer: Why does this matter going forward, for your next class, a future job, or your life?
A strong answer

Includes all five steps, uses "I" in the Action, and sounds like you.

Question 3

Check It with the 4Cs

Reread your draft using the 4C Check and answer honestly.

  • Clarity and Context: Is your Situation clear, or does it need more detail?
  • Credibility: Is everything in your Action and Result accurate, or did you round up?
  • Consequences: Does your Transfer say something specific, or could it apply to anyone?

Then revise at least one part of your draft, and say what you changed.

A strong answer

Answers honestly, and shows a real revision to at least one step.

Optional: AI thinking partner

If you'd like help thinking through your draft, try this prompt in Microsoft Copilot or another AI tool:

Try this prompt

"Help me think through a STAR(T) story for a college reflection assignment. Here's my draft: [paste]. Using the 4C Check (Clarity, Context, Credibility, Consequences), what's one part of my story that could be clearer, more specific, or more accurate? Ask me questions. Don't rewrite it for me."

Use what's useful. AI can help you think it through. It can't tell your story for you.

Keep it general

Leave out your full name, where you live, and other identifying details, including other people's names in your story.

How it's scored

Full credit goes to honest, specific reflection in Part I and a STAR(T) draft that's clearly in your own voice, with real evidence of revision after the 4C Check.
