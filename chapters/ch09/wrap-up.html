Wrap Up

Module 9 Wrap Up

Pull it together before you head back to your course.

About 2 minutes 8 of 8 in this module

You've reached the end of Module 9. Here's what to carry forward.

Key takeaways

  • Critical thinking is thinking on purpose. Pause, ask one good question, then decide.
  • Notice what's shaping your thinking. Assumptions, biases, and thought traps feel true in the moment, but they're rarely accurate.
  • Use the 4C Check. Clarity: what am I really deciding? Context: what matters most? Credibility: what do I know, and what am I assuming? Consequences: what might happen next?
  • STAR(T) is a story about you. Use "I" in the Action, name a real Result, and make the Transfer specific.
  • The 4Cs make your story stronger. They catch vague setups, rounded-up results, and generic Transfers.
  • AI can ask the questions. You tell the story. Check what AI says, and keep your story in your own voice.
Reflect

What's one decision coming up where you'll try the 4C Check, and which C do you think will matter most?
