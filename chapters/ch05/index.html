Why this matters

Financial stress is one of the biggest, and least talked about, barriers to college success. It isn't just uncomfortable. It's mentally expensive: your brain keeps working on money problems in the background, even when you're trying to focus on something else.

This module won't tell you to follow a strict budget or stop buying coffee. It will help you see your financial situation more clearly, and give your money a plan that fits your real life. That's the only honest starting point.

What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module:

  1. A

    Objective A: I can build a realistic zero-based budget that assigns all available income to specific categories.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #5
  2. B

    Objective B: I can identify at least one pattern in my spending habits and describe one small change I want to make.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #5
  3. C

    Objective C: I can explain what financial wellness means and describe how it connects to my overall wellbeing.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #5
  4. D

    Objective D: I can use AI tools safely for budgeting exploration without sharing personal financial information.

    You'll show this in Mile Marker #5
