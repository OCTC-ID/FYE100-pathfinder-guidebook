Dollar Tracker

Look back: where your money actually goes.

About 5 minutes 1 of 7 in this module

Most people think they know where their money goes, until they actually look.

The Dollar Tracker is a reality check, not a budget. Write down where your money went, big and small, with no judging. Choose the option that fits your situation. Both give you the same thing: a clearer picture of your spending patterns. Rough estimates are fine.

Your numbers stay with you

What you type is saved only in this browser, on this device. It isn't sent anywhere. Rounded or practice numbers work just as well as exact ones.
