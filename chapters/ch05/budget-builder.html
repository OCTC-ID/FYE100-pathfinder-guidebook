Budget Builder

Look ahead: give every dollar a job.

About 10 minutes 2 of 7 in this module

A zero-based budget gives every dollar coming in this month a job, until there are zero dollars left without one.

Saving is a job. Paying down debt is a job. Fun is a job too. The point isn't to spend less on everything. It's to decide on purpose, before the money is gone, instead of wondering at the end of the month where it went.

Your numbers stay with you

What you type is saved only in this browser, on this device. It isn't sent anywhere. Rounded or practice numbers work just as well as exact ones.
