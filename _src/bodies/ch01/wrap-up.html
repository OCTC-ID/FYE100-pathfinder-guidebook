Key takeaways

  • In college, responsibility moves to you. The support is there, but you start the conversation, and you reach out before a deadline, not after.
  • Your syllabus is a contract. Read the policies, note every deadline, and ask when something is unclear.
  • The 10 Essential Skills are your map. They describe what college should build in you, and you'll track your own growth on them.
  • Sounding right isn't the same as being right. AI completes patterns; it doesn't check facts. How you use it matters more than whether you use it.
  • AI rules differ by course and assignment. Check the AI Course Policy and each assignment's level of AI use, use the 3 Qs, and keep a trail.
  • Know your tools. Blackboard and Office 365 are how your work gets done and seen. Learn them early.
