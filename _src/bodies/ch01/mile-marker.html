Question 1

Why did you choose to attend college, and OCTC specifically?

What motivated you to take this step? Was it career goals, personal growth, a fresh start, family, stability, or something else? What made OCTC the right place?

A strong answer

Names at least one specific reason you chose college, and one reason you chose OCTC.

Question 2

What does success look like for you this semester, in your own terms?

Consider grades, habits, balance, confidence, exploring careers, or something entirely personal to you.

A strong answer

Defines success in your own words, and connects it to something you actually want this semester.

Question 3

What is one strength, habit, or quality you already have that will help you reach your goals?

Think about what you already bring to the table: work ethic, persistence, curiosity, communication skills, resilience, or something else. How will it support you this semester?

A strong answer

Names one strength and explains how it will help you this semester, ideally with an example of when it has helped you before.

Question 4

What is one goal you want to accomplish before this semester is over, and what are two concrete steps you'll take to reach it?

Be specific. "Do better" isn't a goal. "Pass my math class" or "meet with my Success Coach twice" is a goal.

A strong answer

States one specific, realistic goal, names at least two concrete steps you'll take, and says what success will look like when you reach it.

[[tip]] Pathfinder Tip

A goal is specific when someone else could tell whether you reached it. A step is concrete when you could put it on your calendar.

Question 5

Where does AI already show up in your life, and how do you feel about using it for school right now?

Name at least two places you already use or run into AI. They can be obvious, like a chatbot, or hidden, like autocorrect or your recommended videos. Then tell us where you stand today. Maybe you're excited, unsure, skeptical, or you'd rather not use it at all. All of those are fair answers. If you can, name one way AI could support your learning without doing the learning for you.

A strong answer

Names at least two specific examples of AI in your daily life and describes honestly how you feel about using it for school. There's no right position here, only a clear one.
