Every KCTCS student currently enrolled in a course can install the full version of Office 365 for free, on up to five devices: PC, Mac, Apple phones and tablets, and Android phones and tablets.

The software keeps working as long as you're enrolled in at least one class at a KCTCS college. That includes Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Teams, and OneDrive.

Install on a PC or Mac

  1. Sign in to your KCTCS Office 365 account.
  2. Select Install Office.
  3. If you're asked to save or run the file, choose Run.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to install Office.
  5. After Office is installed, if you're asked to sign in, use your KCTCS email address and password.

Install on a phone or tablet

  1. Install the Microsoft Office app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the app and sign in with your KCTCS student email and password.

What's included

Word
Papers, reports, and assignments. The standard for academic writing.
Excel
Spreadsheets, budgets, and data tracking.
PowerPoint
Presentations and slides for class projects.
OneDrive
Cloud storage, so your files are on every device you use.
OneNote
A digital notebook for notes, ideas, and class materials.
Microsoft Copilot
The AI assistant in your Office 365 account. Sign in with your student login.

Need help installing or signing in? Contact OCTC Information Technology Services.
