Key takeaways

  • You're already in community. Home, work, class, teams, and online spaces all count, and you're already shaping how they work.
  • Every community runs on a system. Look for the people, the rules (especially the unwritten ones), who has influence, and the leverage points.
  • Small and real beats big and vague. Naming a pattern out loud, to the people already in the room, is civic engagement too.
  • Know your offer and your boundary. Say what you can bring, and what you're not available for, before you get more involved.
  • Your stories are already here. Pick your four STAR(T) skills, draft one, and say it out loud.
