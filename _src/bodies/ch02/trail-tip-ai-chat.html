Before you open your first AI Chat in this course, here's something most people never get to see. It isn't a secret. Nobody usually takes the time to explain it. The AI Chats in this course aren't a generic chatbot pointed at you. Each one was set up inside Blackboard for a specific moment in your learning, with a specific purpose.

What is a large language model?

Tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, and the AI Chats in this course are all powered by a large language model: an AI system trained on enormous amounts of text, so it can predict and generate language that sounds clear and relevant.

On its own, it's a very capable generalist. It has read more text than any person ever could, and it can discuss almost any topic. But it doesn't know who you are, what class you're in, or what you're supposed to be thinking about today. Until someone gives it direction, it's just potential.

The AI isn't smart on its own. It's smart in response to what it's been told.

Lesson 1.3: What's AI, Really?

The part nobody shows you: the setup

Before you type your first message, the AI has already read a set of instructions you don't see. Those instructions tell it how to behave in this specific chat.

For the AI Chats in this course, the instructions come from the setup in Blackboard: the type of chat, the topic, the coach's name and personality, how it should respond, and how complex its answers should be. Blackboard turns those choices into instructions the AI reads first, and they shape everything that happens next.

System prompt: the hidden instructions an AI reads before a conversation starts. Almost every AI tool has one.

Module 4

What this means for you

  1. It has a job to do

    The AI Chats aren't here to answer every question you have. Each one has a purpose, usually to help you think, not to think for you. If it keeps asking you questions instead of giving answers, that's not a glitch. That's the point.

  2. Your instructor can see the conversation

    After you submit, your instructor can review the whole chat. That's how you get credit, and how your instructor learns whether the activity is working. Have an honest conversation.

  3. It can still be wrong

    Even with good instructions, the AI can misunderstand you, wander off track, or say something that doesn't land. Each chat ends with a reflection question where you can say what worked and point out anything that seemed wrong or biased.

AI Literacy BE Safe BE Critical

The AI Chats use Blackboard's AI Conversation tool, which runs on Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service. According to Blackboard, your conversation isn't used to retrain the AI. It's saved with your assignment, where your instructor can review it.

The same rule applies here as with any AI tool: don't share other people's personal information, passwords, or anything private in the chat.

Next time you use any AI tool, ask yourself: what was this told to do before I showed up? The answer shapes everything you're about to experience. That question is the beginning of AI literacy.

AI isn't magic. It's instructions, context, and a very capable language engine pointed in a specific direction by a person with a goal. In this course, the goal is your learning. Now go have an honest conversation.
