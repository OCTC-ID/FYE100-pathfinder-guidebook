Key takeaways

  • No one does this alone. The students who struggle most are usually the ones who wait too long to ask, not the ones who know the least.
  • Interpersonal Relations starts with self-awareness. How you show up with other people shapes what help you ask for, and what help you get.
  • Know your Success Coach before you need them. Save their contact information and introduce yourself early.
  • Help-seeking is a skill. Ask early, be specific, communicate professionally, and follow through.
  • OCTC has more support than most students use. Tutoring, counseling, accessibility, TRIO, the library, and IT are all there for you.
  • Your email is part of your professional reputation. Write a clear subject line, use a greeting, name your course, ask a clear question, and close politely.
