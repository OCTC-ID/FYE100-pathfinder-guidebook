Question 1

Who are 3–5 people or resources that can help you succeed this semester?

Think about family, friends, mentors, coworkers, instructors, advisors, tutors, your Success Coach, TRIO, Counseling, the Library, and other OCTC resources. For each one, explain specifically how they can support you.

A strong answer

Names at least three people or resources, explains specifically how each one helps, and includes your Success Coach by name.

Question 2

What are two specific ways you'll use your support network intentionally this semester?

Describe two specific strategies. Not just "I'll reach out when I need help," but when, how, and for what. Which connections do you want to strengthen?

A strong answer

Describes two specific ways you'll use your network, each with when, how, and for what you'll reach out. It shows the difference between knowing support exists and actually using it.

Question 3

What is one new campus resource you want to explore?

Consider the Counseling Center, Accessibility Services, Advising, your Success Coach, the Library, the Teaching & Learning Center (tutoring), TRIO, Student Life and clubs, Career Services, or Financial Aid. Choose one that feels relevant to your goals or situation right now, and explain why.

A strong answer

Names a resource you haven't used yet, explains what it offers and how it connects to your goals, and names one concrete next step for getting there.
