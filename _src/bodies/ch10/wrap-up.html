Key takeaways

  • Showing up isn't the same as collaborating. Milestone collaboration makes the group better: ideas that fit the goal, conflict handled directly, fair roles, and trust through change.
  • Know your default mode. Starter, Organizer, Analyzer, Doer, Encourager, Quiet Worker, Big-Picture Thinker. Most people are a mix.
  • Your type is a map, not a rulebook. Use it to understand your tendencies, not to box yourself in.
  • Every strength has a struggle to watch for. These are style differences, not flaws, and every team needs all kinds.
  • Say what you need. One clear, respectful sentence can prevent a week of frustration.
  • Let AI hand out the to-do list, not do the work. A plan only helps if your group follows through.
