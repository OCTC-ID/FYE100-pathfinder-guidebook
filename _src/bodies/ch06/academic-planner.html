An academic plan isn't a contract. It's a rough map of where you're headed and what has to happen in what order.

Start rough and revise it with your Success Coach. Fill in your program of interest, the things to know before you plan, and your next term plus a couple more. Then bring it, and your questions, to your advising appointment.

[[see]] Look ahead

Lesson 6.4: Build a Simple Academic Plan walks you through how to think about each part.

Fill in your plan

Your plan saves in this browser as you go. Credit hours add up for each term. Select Save as PDF to print it or bring it to your appointment. You may attach it to Mile Marker #6 if you like, but you don't have to.

[[tip]] Pathfinder Tip

Look up exact course names and requirements in the KCTCS catalog. Rough is fine for now. Your Success Coach will help you fill in the gaps.
