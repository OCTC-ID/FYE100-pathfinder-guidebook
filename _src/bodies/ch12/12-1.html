Final review

Before you submit, run each story through this list.

  • [[tick]]All five steps are there. Situation, Task, Action, Result, and Transfer.
  • [[tick]]The Action is yours. You say "I," not just "we," and you name what you actually did.
  • [[tick]]The skill is evident, not just stated. Someone listening could name the skill even if you didn't.
  • [[tick]]The Transfer is specific. You say how and where this skill shows up next: your next classes, a job, your life. Not just "this will help me."
  • [[tick]]It sounds like you. Not a template, and not AI-generated.
  • [[tick]]Your fourth story explains your choice. Start it by naming the skill and why you chose it.

Submitting your stories

  • Submit your stories in the Capstone module in Blackboard, one response for each skill. Check Blackboard for the due date.
  • Record each one (video or audio) or type it directly into the submission box. Tell It Out Loud walks you through recording.
  • Recordings are never scored on delivery, only on the story and its structure.
[[tip]] Pathfinder Tip

Say each story out loud once before you record it. The recording isn't the performance. The preparation is.

Your stories, your voice

AI can ask you questions that help you remember details or tighten a step. It can't know what happened to you, and a story AI wrote won't sound like you, because it isn't.

Use AI the way the assignment's AI use label allows, and tell the story yourself.
