FYE 100 ends before your other courses do. Finish the rest of the semester the way you finished this one.

Once your STAR(T) Stories are in, you're done with FYE 100. Your other classes keep going, often through finals. A few things to keep in mind for that stretch:

Heading into finals

  1. Check every syllabus now

    Finals look different across classes. Some are a single test, some are comprehensive, and some are a final paper or project. Find out what each one is, and when, so nothing catches you off guard.

  2. Go back to your time plan

    The time and energy strategies from Module 3 matter most in the final stretch of the semester. Map your week, and watch your batteries.

  3. Use your support team

    FYE 100 has ended, but you can keep working with your Success Coach. The people and resources from Module 2 are still available, too. Asking for help at the end of the semester is exactly as valid as asking at the beginning. The Lookout below lists decisions your coach can help with now.

  4. Know your grades

    Check where you stand in each class, and what a final can and can't change. The grades Trail Tip shows how to figure it out. If a final can't raise your grade enough, talk with your Success Coach about your next steps.

Lookout: Ask Your Success Coach

Several big decisions come up near the end of the semester. Talk with your Success Coach before finals start, while you have the most options. Your coach can help with:

  • Withdrawing from a course, and what it would mean for your plan and your financial aid
  • Asking an instructor about an incomplete, if something outside your control has kept you from finishing
  • What a final grade means for your program, if your program has admission or grade requirements
  • Registering for next semester, if you haven't yet
