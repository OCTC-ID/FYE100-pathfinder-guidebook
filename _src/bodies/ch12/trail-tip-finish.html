FYE 100 ends before your other courses do. Finish the rest of the semester the way you finished this one.

Once your STAR(T) Stories are in, you're done with FYE 100. Your other classes keep going, often through finals. A few things to keep in mind for that stretch:

Heading into finals

  1. Check every syllabus now

    Finals look different across classes. Some are a single test, some are comprehensive, and some are a final paper or project. Find out what each one is, and when, so nothing catches you off guard.

  2. Go back to your time plan

    The time and energy strategies from Module 3 matter most in the final stretch of the semester. Map your week, and watch your batteries.

  3. Use your support team

    Your Success Coach and the people and resources from Module 2 are still there. Asking for help at the end of the semester is exactly as valid as asking at the beginning.

  4. Know your grades

    Check where you stand in each class, and what a final can and can't change. The grades Trail Tip shows how to figure it out. If a final can't change enough, your Success Coach can help you plan what comes next.

[[owl]]

Lookout: Ask Your Success Coach

If a class isn't going the way you hoped, talk with your Success Coach before finals, not after grades post. There may be more options now than there will be later.

Your coach can help you think through what comes next, including what it means for next semester and your financial aid.
