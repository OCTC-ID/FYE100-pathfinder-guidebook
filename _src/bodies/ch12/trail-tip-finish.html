Once your STAR(T) Stories are in, you're done with FYE 100. But your other classes may still have weeks to go, often including finals. A few things to keep in mind for that stretch:

Heading into finals

  1. Check every syllabus now

    Finals look different across classes. Some are a single test, some are comprehensive, and some are a final paper or project. Find out what each one is, and when, so nothing catches you off guard.

  2. Go back to your time plan

    The time and energy strategies from Module 3 matter most in the last weeks of the term. Map your week, and watch your batteries.

  3. Use your support team

    The people and resources from Module 2 are still there. Asking for help at the end of the term is exactly as valid as asking at the beginning.

  4. Know your grades

    Check where you stand in each class, and what a final can and can't change. The grades Trail Tip shows how to figure it out.
