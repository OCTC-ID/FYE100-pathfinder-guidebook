Question 1

What study strategy, habit, or academic tool did you focus on this week, and why did you choose it?

Be specific about what it is and what made it worth trying. "I studied more" isn't specific. "I tried reviewing my notes out loud the night before class" is.

A strong answer

Names one specific strategy, habit, or tool and gives a real reason you chose it.

Question 2

How did you actually use it, and what did you notice?

Did it help you understand, remember, or stay focused? Did anything surprise you, good or bad? Be honest about what happened, not what you hoped would happen.

A strong answer

Describes how you used it in a real class or task and at least one specific thing you noticed about your learning.

Question 3

After this module, what's one thing you want to keep doing differently, and why does it feel realistic for your actual life right now?

This isn't asking for a perfect plan. It's asking for one honest intention that fits where you are right now.

A strong answer

Names one specific change and explains why it fits your life as it really is right now.

Question 4

Think about any AI tool you used this week, for this course or any other. Where would your use land on the AI Creep Meter?

Closer to brainstorming, closer to drafting or rewriting, or somewhere in between? Was that where you meant to land, or did it shift further than you planned? If you didn't use AI at all this week, that's a complete answer too. Just say so.

A strong answer

Says honestly where your AI use landed (or that you didn't use AI) and whether that was where you meant to land. Full credit doesn't depend on where you landed on the meter.
