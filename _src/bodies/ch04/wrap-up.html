Key takeaways

  • You already have a toolkit. OneNote, OneDrive, Teams, and Copilot come with your KCTCS account. Know they're there so you can reach for the right one.
  • Study to make it stick, not to feel busy. Retrieval practice, spaced practice, asking why, and mixing it up beat rereading, highlighting, and cramming.
  • When in doubt about recording, ask. Check the syllabus, ask your instructor first, and respect your classmates' privacy, especially with AI note-takers.
  • SIFT before you trust. Stop, investigate the source, find better coverage, and trace claims, including everything AI tells you.
  • Set your AI boundary at the start. Decide what role AI plays before you open the chat, and be able to say honestly where on the Creep Meter your use landed.
