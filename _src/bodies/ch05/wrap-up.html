Key takeaways

  • You already reason with numbers. Quantitative Reasoning is thinking clearly with numbers in real life, not doing math for a test.
  • Financial wellness is a direction, not a dollar amount. It means feeling steadier because you know where your money goes, at any income.
  • Money stress is real mental load. It affects focus, memory, and planning. If it's getting in the way of school, reach out for help.
  • See it before you change it. Track where your money actually goes, notice what surprises you, and find the leaks that don't match what you value.
  • Give every dollar a job. A zero-based budget assigns all the money coming in, including savings, until nothing is left without a plan.
  • Keep your numbers private with AI. Use AI to think and ask questions, never with real account numbers, income, or debt amounts.
