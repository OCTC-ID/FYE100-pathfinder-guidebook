Key takeaways

  • Professionalism is a pattern, not a performance. It's being someone people can count on, including owning it when things go wrong.
  • Priorities first, schedule second. Name your Musts before you touch the calendar, and leave white space for real life.
  • Protect your best hour. Put your most important work where your energy is highest, and put the phone out of reach.
  • Multitasking is really task-switching. Every switch has a cost. Noticing it is the first step to protecting your focus.
  • Manage your energy, not just your time. Know which of your five batteries is lowest, and do one small thing about it before it runs out.
  • Your brain is the one you have. Learn how it works, use strategies that fit it, and reach out for support when you need it.
