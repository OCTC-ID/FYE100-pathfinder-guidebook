Key takeaways

  • Come with something. Leave with something. Milestone Communication means listening closely, writing clearly, and bringing your own thinking to professional conversations.
  • Career fit has many parts. Interests, strengths, values, work environment, life management, and education all count, not just pay or how a job sounds.
  • Start with the life you want. The career is the tool. The life is the point.
  • Use the right tool for the job. Career Coach for the local picture, O*NET OnLine for what the work actually involves.
  • Honest beats impressive. Naming friction and open questions is part of a good fit analysis, not a sign you chose wrong.
  • Get your facts from sources, not AI. Use AI to question your thinking. Check every number against your research.
  • Register early. Meet with your Success Coach and register as soon as your registration window opens.
