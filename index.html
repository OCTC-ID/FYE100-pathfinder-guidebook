Guides

Reference guides you'll use across the whole course. The Instructor Guide is for faculty and course reviewers.

  • Merit GuideEverything you need for your three Merit activities: setting up your page, finding an activity, what proof to include, and writing your reflection.
  • STAR(T) Stories GuideYour capstone: four true stories that show four Essential Skills. The framework, how to find your stories, how to record them, and a practice guide for each.
  • Instructor GuideFor FYE 100 instructors and reviewers: how the book works, the course map, and how the course aligns to the 10 Essential Skills, AI literacy, and Appreciative Advising.
